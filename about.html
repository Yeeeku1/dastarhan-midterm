Our Story

At the heart of every Kazakh home is a table with space for one more.

The spirit of dastarhan

Hospitality is our first ingredient.

A dastarhan is the table laid out for guests: generous, welcoming, and never in a hurry. We built this restaurant around that simple idea.

Our kitchen brings together familiar Kazakh flavors, seasonal produce, and contemporary cooking. Every plate is made with care, and every guest is welcomed like an old friend.

  • Seasonal ingredients and thoughtful sourcing
  • Recipes inspired by Kazakh home cooking
  • A relaxed place for celebrations and everyday meals
